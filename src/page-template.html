      I'm not technical. Can I still learn this?

      Yes. Most business owners who come to us aren't technical. Because it's one-to-one, your trainer starts from where you are and explains everything using your own business.

      Is it really one-to-one? No batch at all?

      Yes. There are no group batches in this program. Every session is just you and your trainer.

      Can I learn only social media marketing?

      Yes. Choose a social-media-only course covering Instagram, Facebook, paid social ads and WhatsApp, and add Google Ads or SEO later if you want.

      Will I work on my own business accounts?

      Yes, that's the whole idea. You practise on your own Instagram, Facebook, Google Business Profile, website and ad accounts, so what you learn is ready to use.

      Do I need a website?

      No. Many businesses grow with Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps alone. If you have a website, we'll include it in your course.

      Will you run the ads for me?

      This is training, not an agency service. You'll learn to set up and run campaigns yourself, with your trainer guiding each step.
